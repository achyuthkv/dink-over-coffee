Weekly Pickleball Sessions in Bangalore

We meet every Tuesday evening, Thursday evening, and Sunday morning for pickleball games followed by coffee. Whether you're a beginner picking up a paddle for the first time or an advanced player looking for competitive rallies — there's a spot for you.

Why Join Our Pickleball Community?

  • No partner needed — show up solo, we rotate partners every game
  • All levels welcome — beginners get coaching tips, advanced players get competitive matches
  • Consistent schedule — 4-5 sessions per week across mornings and evenings
  • More than a sport — pickleball + coffee + conversations = real friendships

Pickleball in Jayanagar & Basavangudi

Our courts are located in the heart of South Bengaluru. Convenient for anyone in Jayanagar, Basavangudi, JP Nagar, BTM Layout, and surrounding areas. After every session, we grab coffee together — because the best games lead to the best conversations.

How It Works

  1. Sign up for an upcoming session on our website
  2. Show up at the venue — we handle court bookings and group formation
  3. Play pickleball, meet new people, stay for coffee

Casual Games & DUPR Rated Sessions

We offer both casual pickleball sessions for social play and DUPR-rated sessions for players who want to track their competitive rating. Join the Dink Over Coffee DUPR club to log matches and see your progress.
